About Us

Jinhua Li Electric Trading Co., Ltd

Jinhua Li Electric Trading Co., Ltd is a leading manufacturer in the field of electric motors, lithium-ion batteries and energy storage products, with state-of-the-art production and assembly lines and a well-equipped laboratory for product testing and research.

We are committed to providing high-quality electric mobility and energy storage solutions for customers worldwide. Our product range includes electric motorcycles, electric bicycles and LiFePO4 lithium battery series, exported to North America, South America, East Asia, South Asia and beyond.

Located in Jindong District, Jinhua City, Zhejiang Province — adjacent to Yiwu International Trade City with convenient logistics — we support CBU / CKD / SKD shipping methods. Dealers worldwide are welcome to contact us for cooperation.
