The atelier

Made in Jinhua, finished by hand.

AURELLE is the jewellery line of Jinhua Li Electric Trading Co., Ltd., an export house on Jinou Road that has been shipping out of Zhejiang for years. The silver is cast and plated by specialist workshops in the province; the checking, photographing and packing happen in our own studio.

We keep the range small on purpose. Six pieces means each one can be worn, repaired and re-ordered — not rotated out of a catalogue every season.

S925 / S999Sterling and fine silver, with the grade stated on every product page.
Hand finishPolished, plated and inspected piece by piece, not by the tray.
WorldwideQuoted shipping to your address before any payment is taken.
