Clover Turquoise Studs

E02 · S925 sterling silver $11.71

Four petals, one turquoise heart.

A 6.5 mm clover of turquoise and zircon, set in S925 silver and finished in champagne gold.

Each petal is a turquoise cabochon framed by a line of tiny zircons; the four leaves meet at a single centre stone. At 6.5 mm it reads as a small point of colour rather than a flower on the ear.

The stud sits flat on a 0.8 mm post with a butterfly back, so it stays in place under hair, headphones and a scarf.
