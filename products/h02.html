Meridian Link Bracelet

H02 · S925 sterling silver $177.14

A ribbon of polished links.

A 5 mm flat-link chain in rhodium-plated S925 silver — the piece you put on and leave on.

Each link is polished on both faces and hinged so the bracelet folds flat against the wrist. At 6.0–6.2 g it has enough presence to be worn alone and enough flexibility to sit under a cuff.

Fifteen centimetres, finished with a fold-over clasp. Because the links are flat rather than round, the surface reads as a continuous ribbon of light rather than a series of separate rings.

Materials & care

Silver darkens with air, skin and perfume. Wipe with the soft cloth included and store in the pouch — not in the bathroom.

Plated surfaces wear first where they rub: put the piece on last, take it off before sport, swimming or cleaning.
