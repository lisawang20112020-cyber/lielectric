Before you order

Shipping & payment

Shipping

Orders are packed in Jinhua and dispatched within 3–5 business days after payment clears. We ship with tracked international couriers and choose the service by destination; the tracking number is emailed when the parcel leaves.

Shipping is quoted for your address before any payment is taken. Place the order, and the quote arrives with the payment link — if it does not suit you, the order is cancelled and nothing is charged.

Payment

Online — PayPal or card

After you place the order you can pay on the confirmation page with PayPal or a Visa/Mastercard — no PayPal account is needed for card payment. Shipping is quoted together with the payment.

Offline — bank transfer (T/T)

Pay in USD or EUR to the company account at Bank of China, Jinhua branch, Jindong sub-branch. Use the order number as the transfer reference.

If something is wrong

Write to us within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the piece and its packaging. If it arrived damaged or is not what you ordered, we will repair, replace or refund it.

Frequently asked questions

When will my order ship?

We pack in Jinhua and dispatch within 3–5 business days after payment clears. The tracking number is emailed as soon as the parcel leaves.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes — we ship with tracked international couriers to nearly every country, choosing the service by destination.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is quoted for your address after you place the order. The quote arrives together with the payment link — if it is not for you, the order is cancelled and nothing is charged.

Which payment methods do you accept?

PayPal or card through a secure payment link, or a bank transfer in USD or EUR. Alipay and WeChat Pay are available on request.

What are the pieces made of?

Every piece is S925 sterling or S999 fine silver, made in small batches in Jinhua, Zhejiang.

What if something arrives wrong?

Write to us within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the piece and its packaging — we repair, replace or refund.
